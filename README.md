# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard
An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze retail revenue, customer traffic, and product performance across store locations. Designed with a custom espresso/beige theme focused on executive readability and data-driven insights.

### 💡 What This Dashboard Tracks

* **KPI Summary (Top Header):** Quick-glance metrics for **Total Sales**, **Total Transactions**, and **Total Units Sold**.
* **Interactive Slicers (Left Sidebar):** Filter all visual data instantly by **Store Location** and **Product Category**.
* **Core Analytics :**
  * **Sales by Location:** Revenue distribution across store branches.
  * **Sales by Category:** Product mix breakdown (% share).
  * **Monthly Revenue:** Sales trends and growth over time.
  * **Peak Hours:** Hourly transaction traffic to pinpoint rush periods.
* **Top Performers (Right Table):** A custom PivotTable highlighting the **Top 10 Product Types** by transaction quantity and total sales.

* **Excel:** PivotTables, Slicer Connections, Dynamic Charting, Advanced Formatting.
